Say hello to Yinyue.

A music player that lives in your tray, not on your taskbar. Press a hotkey, search your Jellyfin server or your local files, play, and it gets out of the way again.

Made to be summoned, not stared at.

Yinyue starts with Windows and waits in the tray. Everything is reachable from the keyboard, and most of it works without ever showing the window.

Summon overlay

One global hotkey brings up a small panel anchored to a corner of your screen. It appears, you act, it dismisses.

Jellyfin, direct

Streams straight from your server with no transcoding when the format allows. Favourites, playlists and albums included.

Local files too

Point it at a folder and it indexes what is there. Local and remote results appear together, duplicates folded.

Keyboard first

Every action has a shortcut, and every shortcut can be rebound. Tap for the small thing, hold for the bigger one.

Search that narrows

Type album:, playlist:, fav: or queue: in front of a search to look in exactly one place.

A queue you can shape

Add, remove, reorder and jump, all from the keyboard. Shuffle and loop modes follow you across sessions.

Media keys and the OS

Play, pause and skip from your keyboard's media keys. Windows shows what is playing, with artwork.

Lightweight by design

Idle at zero CPU with a small footprint, all day. A resident app should never be the reason your laptop fan spins.

What it looks like.

The whole app is a few small panels that stack above one another.

Get Yinyue.

Free and open source. Installs for the current user only, so it never asks for administrator rights.
